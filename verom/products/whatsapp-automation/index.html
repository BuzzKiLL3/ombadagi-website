Concept

How a workflow is meant to run

Concept illustration of the planned workflow.

  1. 01

    Customer message

    A customer who has opted in sends an enquiry or replies.

  2. 02

    Automation workflow

    Rules read the message and decide what should happen next.

  3. 03

    Business system

    The relevant record is created or updated where your team works.

  4. 04

    Action or notification

    The right person, or the customer, is informed.

Use cases

Use cases we are designing for

  • Lead capture

    Turn inbound enquiries into structured lead records.

  • Enquiry routing

    Send each enquiry to the right person or team.

  • Follow-up reminders

    Remind your team, and opted-in customers, when a follow-up is due.

  • Order and quotation notifications

    Notify customers when an order or quotation changes status.

  • Maintenance reporting

    Let field or shop-floor staff report issues in a structured way.

  • Voice notes to records

    A possible future workflow: turning voice notes into structured data.

Requirements

What every workflow depends on

  • Access to an official business messaging platform and its approved APIs.
  • Customer opt-in and consent, following the platform’s rules and applicable law.
  • Approved message templates where the platform requires them.
  • Integration access to the business systems involved.
Automation never replaces consent requirements or platform policies. Capabilities will be confirmed per project.
