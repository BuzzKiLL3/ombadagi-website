Workflow

How it works

From the customer’s enquiry to an issued quotation, in four structured steps.

  1. Capture the enquiry

    Record the customer’s requirements in a structured intake instead of free-form notes.

  2. Calculate with your rules

    Your sizing and estimating rules run as configured formulas, with a trace of every result.

  3. Cost and price

    Quantities roll into material, labour and process costs, then your pricing rules apply.

  4. Issue the quotation

    Move the quotation through its statuses and produce a PDF document for the customer.
