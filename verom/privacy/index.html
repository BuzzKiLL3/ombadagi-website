Privacy Policy

Starter text. It reflects how this website works today and must be reviewed by a qualified adviser before you rely on it.

Who we are

This website is operated by Verom Solutions, a business registered in India under the Udyam (MSME) registration system. Registered details are listed on the About page once verified.

Contact for privacy questions: ombadagi@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, business email, company, country, the product you are interested in and your message. Trial and interest forms may also ask for an approximate quotation volume and intended use, both optional.

Our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, arrange a trial you requested, or contact you about a product you registered interest in. The basis is your request and your consent given on the form.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who processes it

The website and its form endpoint run on Cloudflare. When form delivery is configured, your submission is passed to an email delivery or storage service so it reaches us. These providers act on our behalf.

Information may be processed outside your country, including in India, the European Union and the United States, depending on the provider.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept as long as needed to respond and follow up, and then deleted. A defined retention period will be stated here after review.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by writing to ombadagi@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act may give you further rights, including complaining to a supervisory authority.
