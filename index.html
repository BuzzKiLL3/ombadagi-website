Practical digitalisation, built by an engineer who understands the shop floor.

Many industrial companies already have the data they need — inside PLCs, machines, spreadsheets and people's heads. The challenge is turning it into something useful without a huge, risky IT project.

I work on focused, well-scoped solutions: connecting equipment, making performance visible, anticipating failures and automating repetitive tasks. Small steps, measurable value, then scale what works.

Approach

From problem to proven result

A simple four-step method that keeps risk and cost under control.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Visit the site, talk to the people involved, and define the real problem and how success will be measured.

  2. 02

    Build

    Develop a focused, practical solution using the equipment and systems you already have wherever possible.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Test it in real production conditions and check the results against the goals agreed at the start.

  4. 04

    Scale

    Roll out what works to more machines, lines or sites — with documentation your team can maintain.
