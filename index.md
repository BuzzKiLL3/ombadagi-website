## Approach to digitalisation

Sensors and dashboards only pay off when they answer an engineering question: why a spindle runs hot, why a line loses an hour a shift. The work starts from mechanics and process, then adds the data, automation and business systems that make the answer visible and repeatable.

- **Mechanics:** how the machine moves, wears and fails
- **Data:** the few signals that matter
- **Performance:** losses ranked by impact
- **Decisions:** action at the right level

## From problem to proof

1. **Understand:** start from the business or engineering problem, on site, and agree what success looks like.
2. **Measure:** capture how the process or machine behaves today, using existing data first.
3. **Analyse:** separate symptoms from causes with data, process maps and engineering judgement.
4. **Improve:** design the simplest practical change and pilot it on one machine, line or team.
5. **Validate:** compare the pilot with the baseline and decide whether to scale.

No guaranteed savings: results are measured against the baseline agreed at the start.
